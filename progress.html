Completed Work

What we have accomplished so far

These milestones describe project work completed or underway; they do not establish that an industrial exposure caused a cancer outcome.

Site Review

Priority industrial site selected

We selected the KSolv Maritime and Petroleum facility in Channelview, Texas as a priority site for detailed review of emissions information, surrounding cancer patterns, and related public records.

Technical Development

Statewide cancer-data tool built

We created a web application for visualizing county-level cancer data across Texas, with additional data collection and refinement still in progress.

Research

Further investigation framework created

We are reviewing environmental reports, emissions records, legal filings, and cancer-rate patterns to determine which questions and locations warrant deeper research.

Advocacy

Civic and legislative outreach started

We have begun researching relevant public officials, reviewing committee roles and environmental policy positions, and preparing findings for outreach and interviews.

Research note: A geographic or statistical relationship does not by itself establish that an environmental exposure caused a cancer outcome.
