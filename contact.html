Reasons to Reach Out

What can we help with?

Research + Data

Share relevant datasets, research papers, methodology feedback, or information that could improve our analysis.

Advocacy + Policy

Connect with us about legislative outreach, public-policy questions, or opportunities to present project findings.

Community + Media

Share local context, community concerns, interview opportunities, or ideas for making the project more accessible.

Before contacting us

This project investigates possible associations in public data. We do not provide medical advice and do not treat correlation as proof that a specific environmental exposure caused an individual cancer case.
