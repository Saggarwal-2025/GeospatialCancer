Student-Led Research + Advocacy

Investigating Cancer Patterns Across Texas

Texas Cancer Advocacy Project combines data analysis and civic advocacy to investigate cancer patterns across Texas and examine environmental or industrial factors that may deserve further research. Our work explores possible associations and does not assume that correlation proves causation.

What’s Next

Turning early research into a stronger evidence base

The next stage focuses on improving the quality of our analysis, measuring our advocacy work, and connecting with more communities and decision-makers.

01

Benzene-trend analysis

Study available emissions data over time and document what the data can — and cannot — support.

02

Demographic exposure research

Explore how environmental burdens may overlap with population characteristics while avoiding unsupported causal conclusions.

03

County risk-scoring research

Develop a transparent method for prioritizing locations for further investigation rather than labeling communities as definitively unsafe.

04

Advocacy-outcome tracking

Track outreach, responses, interviews, follow-ups, and concrete policy or community outcomes.

05

Community outreach

Share accessible information, gather local context, and build connections with residents, researchers, and organizations.
